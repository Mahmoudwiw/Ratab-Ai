# Ratab AI | رطب

**Know when your body needs water — before you even feel thirsty.**

A smart hydration companion that pairs a weight-sensing cup with an app that tracks real intake, sends calm non-intrusive reminders, and rewards consistency instead of nagging you.

Built for the Creativa Mansoura Hackathon by Team SyntriX (Code 12).

## The Problem

In Dakahlia governorate, the rate of dialysis patients is more than double the global average. It often starts with something as ordinary as forgetting to drink water during a long study or work session — the body's only real alert is the symptom itself, and by then the damage is already done.

## The Solution

- **Weight-sensing cup** — detects real intake automatically, zero manual logging
- **Blue light, not sound** — a calm visual nudge that never breaks focus
- **3 risk levels** — Gentle, Moderate, and Strict reminder intensity, chosen by the user
- **Virtual garden reward** — consistent hydration grows a garden instead of triggering guilt-based nagging
- **Doctor on Demand (phase 2)** — consultations with kidney and hydration specialists
